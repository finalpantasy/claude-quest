## Problem before solution
Observed (the owner, 2026-10-14): "The little Claude crabs are not very readable with all the gear on." In `docs/images/fight.png` the hero is a blob of helmet, wings and robe: the orange shell is mostly covered, the eyes are hidden, wings are as wide and bright as the body, and there is no outline around the composite. The orange follower crab next to it reads more like the hero than the hero does. The task-16 worker flagged the same risk (crab mostly hidden under robe and wings in stills).
Facts: gear is composed onto the crab in `hooks/sprites/gear.ts` (`putHat` ~19, `putMask` ~28, `putChest` ~36, `putBack` ~51) and `hooks/sprites/backs.ts` (`putBack` ~109); the crab body and poses are in `hooks/sprites/crab.ts`; followers are drawn as smaller crabs in `hooks/art.ts` (~217).
## Outcome and scope
A "crab first" rule, applied to every generated piece so the thousands of variants stay:
1. Orange wins: at least 60% of the shell's pixels stay bare orange in every pose with full gear. Chest pieces become a tabard strip, belt, sash or chest plate on the lower or middle shell plus a trim tint on the shell edge, never a full cover.
2. Eyes always visible: hats and helmets sit on top of or between the eye stalks; masks become small face pieces under the eyes (or go behind the stalks), never covering the eye pixels.
3. Back pieces (wings, capes, banners) sit behind the body, no wider than the shell plus 2 cells per side at band size, drawn one or two shades darker or desaturated so they frame the crab instead of competing.
4. A 1-cell dark outline around the whole geared crab composite (not around each piece), so the silhouette separates from the backdrop and from monsters.
5. Value hierarchy: the crab's orange stays the brightest, most saturated large area; gear rarity colors appear as trims and small areas; weapons, auras and upgrade glows carry the spectacle.
6. Followers are never orange crabs: draw them as other small critters (reuse pet or creature sprites in their own colors), clearly smaller than the hero.
7. Keep every count from task-11 (2,000+ weapons, armor pieces, auras): the counting tests must still pass with distinct looks.
Deliverable for review: a before/after sheet `docs/art/samples/readability-before-after.png` (the same 24 random gear combos, before on top, after below, at 1x band size and 3x), then re-render `docs/art/samples/crab-gear.png` and the README images in `docs/images/`.
Non-goals: new gear, game rules, monster art, environment art.
