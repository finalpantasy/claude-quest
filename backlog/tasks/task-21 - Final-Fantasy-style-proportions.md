## Problem before solution
The owner (2026-10-15) on the task-20 32-bit sheet: "too chibi. final fantasy pixel style?" The 32-bit V1 and V8 (about 52 px tall, big head) read as chibi. The owner wants the feel of classic Final Fantasy pixel art: taller proportions, smaller heads, detailed armor and robes, dramatic poses.
Facts: the 32-bit pipeline is proven in task-20: `hooks/sprites/hero-32bit.ts` (shaded shapes, hue-shifted ramps, selective outline), `hooks/png.ts` (pure-TS PNG data URI), `tools/render_hero_options.ts --b32`. Band scene is 720 by 88, so the hero can be up to about 64 px tall. The 8 gear sets are Knight, Wizard, King, Rogue, Samurai, Pirate, Dev, Cleric.
## Outcome and scope
A comparison test, not a rebuild. Four original heroes at 32-bit, each a fair best effort, all inspired by the general era and feel only:
- T1. Tactics proportions: about 4 heads tall, 3/4 view, long limbs, small head, detailed layered armor (the PlayStation tactics-RPG look).
- T2. 16-bit battle-sprite proportions redone at 32-bit detail: about 3 heads tall, side-facing battle stance, strong silhouette.
- T3. Heroic: about 5 heads tall, the hero fills most of the band height, cloak and hair motion.
- T4. The worker's wildcard that best says "classic Final Fantasy" at band size.
Strict originality: no characters, job designs or sprites from Final Fantasy or any other game. In particular the wizard set must not reproduce the iconic pointed-hat-with-shadowed-face-and-glowing-eyes mage, and no recognisable job outfits (dragoon armor, white mage robe with red triangles and similar). Original designs that evoke the era are the goal.
Each gets: bare plus the 8 gear sets (hand-built at this size, as task-20 did), attack and sit poses, 3x and 1x; a random-look row of 8 (skin, hair, eyes); and a band-size fight scene next to today's live crab and the task-20 32-bit V1. Measure bytes per hero and per scene with the PNG method.
Output: `docs/art/samples/hero-ff.png`, `docs/art/samples/hero-ff-scene.png`. Extend `hooks/sprites/hero-32bit.ts` or add `hooks/sprites/hero-ff.ts` (not imported by the game).
Non-goals: wiring into the game, refitting the library, monsters.
