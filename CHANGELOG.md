# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Stats train from the kind of work: edits train Strength, reads and greps Dexterity, shell commands, agents and web calls Intelligence, turns and compactions Vitality, cache reads Luck. A quarter of all training flows to the lowest stat. Models only set the hero's race.
- Band modes: `/quest band strip|pop|full|off`. A one-line strip, a strip that pops to the full scene for about 10 seconds on a level up, legendary or set drop or boss kill (default), the full scene, or nothing.
- Slower long-term pacing: a multi-year climb to 99, tuned for a full workday of Claude, with fast early levels. Life skills follow the same curve. Existing heroes load and keep their level.
- Live work animation: the scene follows what you are doing in Claude.
- Claude Mod Hider support: the band stays out of the way while mods are hidden.
- README gallery with real renders of the mod's own art, produced by `tools/render_scenes.ts`.

## 0.1.0

- First release: an idle ARPG above the Claude Code prompt. Work banks Focus for fights; away from the keyboard the hero camps. Fishing, Mining, Archaeology, Cooking, Smithing and Alchemy. Five acts then the Atlas of Repos. ARPG loot, a `/quest` pane with six tabs. Zero tokens.
