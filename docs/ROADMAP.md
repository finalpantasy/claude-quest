## v0.2.1: gear upgrades (card task-10)
Player outcome: the hero forges its own gear to +1, +2 and up to +15 with ARPG random rolls (critical forges, blessed affixes, milestone bonuses), never losing items. Starts as soon as task-3 merges.

## Art pass, now part of v0.2.0 (D18): task-11 sample sheet (approved 2026-10-13) and task-16 phase B wiring, both merged; task-17 readability pass (crab first) before release
Player outcome: hundreds of distinct monsters and visible gear on the hero, with auras for rarity, sets, relics, legendaries and upgrades, still a small mod.

## v0.2.x: the wandering arsenal (task-12, after task-10; D25)
Player outcome: one permanent crab whose style comes from the loot it finds (titles like Necromancer or Vibe Coder follow its gear), random stat growth, silly fixed birth labels; your work only fuels how much happens.

## v0.2.x: downtime (task-14, after task-12; D26)
Player outcome: breaks put the crab at a campfire and fill a Rested pool; nights and weekends grow crops and send expeditions; a welcome-back summary.

## v0.2.x: random events and humor (task-15, after task-12, can run beside task-14; D27)
Player outcome: funny Godville-style events pop up as a line in the band and fill an event diary.

## v0.2.x: environments and flavor (task-13, after the art wiring and task-12)
Player outcome: 40+ biomes with parallax, weather and day/night, and far more log flavor.

## v0.3: ladder seasons, public leaderboard and async PvP (card task-6, then implementation cards)
Player outcome: an opt-in public profile card and leaderboard with seasonal heroes, no server.
First step: design spec with the owner (what is published, opt-in, how a gist is written without the mod touching tokens), then Sonnet-sized cards.
